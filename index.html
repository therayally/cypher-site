G.H.O.S.T.: powerful AI that never leaves your building.

  • Private AI for sensitive work: your data stays on your own hardware

About G.H.O.S.T.

C.O.S.M.O: the AI-driven cyber intelligence platform.

Cognitive Operations & Security Monitoring Orchestrator. Its AI does the heavy lifting of a security team, from the first alert to the fix, and your analysts approve.

  • Triages the whole alert queue for you: decides what is real, what can be closed and what needs a closer check
  • Assigns the work automatically: the right team and the right person, each with a ready-made work pack
  • Builds rapid-response playbooks and the fixes teams paste straight in: firewall blocks, detection rules, Splunk and Defender searches
  • Takes malware apart with a council of five AI analysts and writes the rules that catch it
  • Shows the reasons behind every score, and nothing changes until a person approves
