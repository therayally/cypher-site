About Cypher

A veteran-owned service partner to the federal government.

Cypher, LLC is a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business, founded in 2009 and headquartered in Ashburn, Virginia.

Who we are

Experience, integrity, performance and expertise.

We put people with deep operational and technical experience on hard customer problems and mission-critical operations.

Cypher is a service partner for the federal government, with technical depth and key personnel in military intelligence, cyber security and operations, software engineering, DevOps, network engineering, systems engineering, aviation safety, enterprise IT management, business process improvement, and agile coaching and engineering.

We blend strategic thinking with tactical execution, and follow industry trends and proven practice, so that our customers' initiatives are delivered. Our staff has decades of experience supporting federal enterprise programs within the DoD, the Intelligence Community and the civilian customer base. Our technical staff is 100% cleared and supports customers onsite.
